# Changelog

## 0.4.1

Fixes for the problems the automated in-game tests found on 2026-10-08 (none of these versions is released).

- Sneak Ambush 0.1.1, Spyglass 0.1.1, Forge Idol Upgrades 0.1.1, Tower Shield Wall 0.1.1, Music Instruments 0.4.1: no
  more false errors and warnings in the log at the main menu (with other item mods installed) and when leaving a
  server that does not have the mod.
- Fishing Fight 0.1.1: reeling on the right side no longer stalls short of the catch; a fish out of the water does
  not fight.
- Swim Dive 0.1.1: a storm wave that washes over you is no longer taken for a dive.
- Creature Morale 0.1.1: a sleeping creature that is afraid of you keeps the sneak-attack bonus.
- Deep North Awakening 0.1.1: lowering the kill target clears the areas that already have that many kills.
- Loot Pickup Filter 0.1.1: a pick that gives 8 items or more is picked up whole.
- Sort Chest 0.1.1: Sort keeps working after a Sort that had nothing to move; the sort order button is mouse only;
  two false warnings are gone; tankards sort under Other.
- Crafting Search and Sort 0.1.1: tankards are listed under Other.
- Creature Kill and Tame Counts 0.1.1: a tame that finishes while you have no character in the world is counted.
- Cultivator Replant 0.1.1: with the mod off, transplants grow on your own setting; with PlantEasily installed a
  transplant can no longer be placed where the game refuses it.
- Tower Shield Wall 0.1.1: an attack press that waited for the bash cooldown ends with the tower shield.
- Spyglass 0.1.1: the zoom you picked comes back after a lower zoom limit.
- Forge Idol Upgrades 0.1.1: a tier with a bad Material setting says why its idols cannot be upgraded; the IDOLS tab
  comes back at once when the mod is turned on with the Forge open.
- Encyclopedia 0.1.1: when its window cannot be built, turning the mod off and on no longer brings the side button
  back.
- Batch Station Feeding 0.1.1: the hover hint follows a station that changes while you keep looking at it.
- Sleep Through the Day 0.1.1: no "Good evening" after a sleep that started at night and ended on the same day.
- Breeding Star Inheritance 0.2.0 and Switchable Lights 0.2.0: the server also refuses a player who has the mod turned
  off, not only one without it (AllowPlayersWithoutMod still lets them stay). Breeding Star Inheritance: its log no
  longer says "Using the server's breeding settings" while the mod is turned off.
- Sailing Skill 0.1.1: the wind icon shows the pull the ship really gets when a game without the mod simulates it;
  the sail's pull no longer swings past its full strength after a wind shift.
- Loot Pickup Filter 0.1.1: the mark no longer covers the hotbar number; the controller lists panel opens beside the
  inventory, not over it.
- Forge Idol Upgrades 0.1.1: the idol tooltip's cost line reads right when a cost is 0; a starred icon that cannot be
  made yet shows the plain icon, not a white square.
- Spyglass 0.1.1: turning EdgeBlur off while looking also stops the work behind the blur.
- Distant Horizons 0.1.1: far trees, rocks and buildings follow the far ground when the mod is turned on while paused
  and after a simulation-distance change; far trees at tile edges stand on the ground again; near far-terrain tiles
  always use exact heights.

## 0.4.0

- Cheated Tag Remover 0.1.0 (new, not released): the console commands `cheated_clear` and `cheated_scan` remove (or
  count) the vanilla cheated tag in a whole world: building pieces, creatures, stations, items in chests, on the
  ground and on stands, and the items you carry.
