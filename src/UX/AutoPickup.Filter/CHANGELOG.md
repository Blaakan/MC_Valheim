# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- Fixed: when one pick gave 8 items or more (for example seed carrots with the Resources world modifier at 3x), the
  items that popped out highest above the plant were treated as filtered items and stayed on the ground. Every item
  of a hand harvest is now picked up, however many there are.
- Fixed: the red or green mark covered the hotbar number (1-8) of marked items in the first row of your inventory.
  The mark now sits on the left edge of the slot, half-way up, where the game shows none of its own slot details.
- Fixed: with a controller, the lists panel (hold RT and click the right stick) opened over the Auto pickup button and
  the left half of your inventory. It now opens to the right of your inventory panel.
- Good to know (nothing changed in the mod): while a chest is open, the game itself uses the right stick click for its
  Place stacks button. The controller gestures of this mod use the same click, so with a chest open they also run
  Place stacks. The README (Controller) explains it; `GamepadControls = false` turns the mod's gestures off.
